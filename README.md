## Concepts


### Stopwords
> A stop word is a commonly used word (such as “the”, “a”, “an”, or “in”) that a search engine has been programmed to ignore, both when indexing entries for searching and when retrieving them as the result of a search query. 

### Stemming vs Lemmatization
> Stemming and Lemmatization are both natural language processing (NLP) techniques used to reduce words to their base or root form. While they aim to achieve a similar goal, they differ in how they approach this task.

- Stemming
Stemming is a process of reducing a word to its root form by chopping off the end of the word. Stemming algorithms are generally rule-based and focus on reducing inflection in words.

- Lemmatization
